Frequently Asked

Help
Is my phone compatible with eSIM? ▼
Most iPhone XS / newer, Samsung S20 / newer, Google Pixel 3 / newer and recent iPads support eSIM. Dial *#06# — if an EID number appears, your device is eSIM ready.
How fast do I receive the QR code? ▼
Instantly. After payment confirmation our team delivers your QR code via WhatsApp or email — usually within 60 seconds, 24 hours a day.
Can I keep my WhatsApp and home number? ▼
Yes. The eSIM handles mobile data only, so your physical SIM stays active for calls and SMS. WhatsApp keeps working with your existing number.
What happens if I use all my data? ▼
You can top up or purchase an additional plan anytime by messaging our consultants. Your eSIM profile stays installed on your device.
Does the eSIM support hotspot / tethering? ▼
Yes, most of our plans support personal hotspot so you can share data with a laptop or travel companion.
